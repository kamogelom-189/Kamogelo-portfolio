KAMOGELO MATHE PORTFOLIO

FILES
- index.html: portfolio content
- style.css: visual styling
- script.js: mobile navigation, copyright year, and profile-photo display

ADD YOUR PROFILE PHOTO
1. Choose a clear, professional photo of yourself.
2. Rename the image file to exactly: profile.jpg
3. Put profile.jpg in the same folder as index.html, style.css, and script.js.
4. Refresh the portfolio. The photo will replace the KM placeholder.

If your image is PNG, either convert it to JPG and name it profile.jpg, or change src="profile.jpg" in index.html to your exact image filename.
